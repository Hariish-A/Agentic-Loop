Social media has changed how young people communicate. Many studies have looked
at this topic and the results are mixed. Some researchers say it is harmful and
others say the effects are small.

One argument is that social media increases anxiety. Teenagers spend a lot of
time on their phones and this may affect sleep. Sleep is important for mental
health. Another point is that social media can help people stay connected with
friends, especially those who live far away.

There are also concerns about comparison. People post their best moments and
this can make others feel worse about their own lives. However, some platforms
have added features to address this.

Overall, social media has both good and bad effects on young people. Parents and
schools should think carefully about how to handle it. More research would help
clarify the situation in the future.
